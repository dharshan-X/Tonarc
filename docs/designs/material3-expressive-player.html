Dynamic Vinyl Waveform Player

Recreated faithfully from your reference image while adhering strictly to Material 3 Expressive web standards: clean dynamic color extraction, rotating grooved vinyl, audio amplitude waveform scrubber, tactile 3D controls, and all unnecessary fake social counters cleanly removed.
